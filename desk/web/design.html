The design system

Colour: a theme is three knobs

Paper (--bg), ink (--fg) and the accent. Every other colour is mixed from them in theme.css: muted text, hairlines, faint fills, the shadow, the desktop's dots. A new theme is one line. Red means one thing everywhere: a hot board, or closing.

Type: two faces, five sizes

Departure Mono is the desk speaking: windows, buttons, labels, numbers. Instrument Sans is for reading: notes, articles, text files. Settings lets either role wear either face. Departure is a pixel font, sharpest at 11, 22 and 44.

Icons

One line style, 2px strokes, the colour of the text around them. Sizes: 14 in rows, 16 in buttons, 20 on the dock, 32 in the launcher, 56 for a missing cover.
